What are the Rules?

To keep the server fun and fair for everyone, please follow these guidelines:


  • No griefing or stealing from other players' bases.
  • No hacking, exploiting, or using unfair modifications.
  • Be respectful to all members in chat and voice.
  • Pranks are allowed, but keep them friendly!

How's the Community?

Our community is friendly, welcoming, and always ready to help new players out. Whether you love massive community projects, trading, or exploring solo, you will find your place here. Join our community spaces to chat, share builds, and stay updated on events!
